**Featured Projects**

**1\. Econometric Analysis of UK House Prices**

**Tools:** Stata, Econometrics, Time-Series Analysis

This project investigates the relationship between UK house prices and macroeconomic indicators, including interest rates, GDP, inflation, unemployment and consumer confidence, using historical data covering 1985–2020. I applied multiple regression, robust standard errors, two-stage least squares (2SLS) and time-series techniques to explore these relationships and interpret the results. The analysis also considers methodological limitations, including non-stationarity, potential endogeneity and the challenges of selecting an appropriate instrumental variable.

**2\. Sainsbury's Financial Analysis & Valuation**

**Tools:** Financial Modelling, Excel, DCF, Scenario Analysis

This project analyses J Sainsbury plc through financial statement forecasting, projected free cash flows and equity valuation. I applied discounted cash flow (DCF) and price-to-earnings valuation approaches, using assumptions around the weighted average cost of capital, terminal growth and future financial performance. Sensitivity analysis and bull, base and bear scenarios were used to examine how changes in key assumptions affect the valuation outcome.

**3\. SQL NYC DCWP Licensing & Enforcement Analysis**

**Tools:** SQL, Data Cleaning, Data Validation, Relational Data Analysis

This project uses three NYC Open Data datasets covering business licences, regulatory inspections and enforcement charges published by the Department of Consumer and Worker Protection (DCWP). Using SQL, I cleaned and profiled the data, removed duplicate records, standardised dates, created a surrogate key for charge records and assessed relationships between datasets while accounting for incomplete matches and one-to-many joins. The analysis investigates licensing patterns, inspection trends, charge activity, repeat enforcement and potential compliance-risk indicators, with recommendations for targeted follow-up and careful interpretation of rates and record counts.

**4\. Python SBA 7(a) vs 504 Loan Performance Analysis**

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Data Analysis, Statistical Hypothesis Testing, Risk Analysis

This project analyses US Small Business Administration (SBA) public loan data to compare the 7(a) and 504 lending programmes, examining observed charge-off rates across industries and geographic markets, alongside financing amounts, repayment terms and business characteristics. Using Python, I cleaned and combined datasets, compared resolved loan outcomes, and applied Mann–Whitney U tests with Bonferroni-adjusted p-values to investigate differences between charged-off and paid-in-full loans. The findings highlight differences in observed outcomes while accounting for sample sizes, missing data, loan maturity and the distinct structures of the two programmes, avoiding unsupported conclusions about causation or inherent credit risk.

**5\. Power BI Apple Retail Performance Dashboard**

**Tools:** Power BI, Data Cleaning, Data Modelling, Data Visualisation, Retail Analytics

This project uses a synthetic Apple retail dataset enriched with real-world product information, including US launch dates, reference prices, product families, generations and product conditions. Using Power BI, the analysis explores sales value, units sold, product-category contribution and growth, alongside store benchmarking, geographic performance and rankings. A key focus is distinguishing revenue growth driven by higher-priced products from genuine sales-volume growth, while accounting for assumptions and limitations in the underlying dataset.
