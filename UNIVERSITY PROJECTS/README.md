## University Projects – Foundations of Data Analytics

My university projects represent the starting point of my data analytics journey, where I developed the analytical thinking, research skills and quantitative techniques that underpin my approach to working with data today.

Through these projects, I explored different approaches to analysing real-world business and economic questions, combining financial analysis, econometric methods, data interpretation and critical evaluation to draw meaningful conclusions and produce evidence-based recommendations.

## Projects

- **Financial Annual Report Analysis – Sainsbury’s FY2024:** Analysed company financial performance and business results to evaluate performance, identify key trends and develop informed business recommendations.
- **Econometrics Report – UK Housing Market:** Applied econometric techniques to investigate relationships within the UK housing market, interpret statistical findings and assess their implications.

## Why These Projects Matter

These projects demonstrate the foundations behind my analytical approach: asking relevant questions, applying appropriate techniques, interpreting results critically and translating findings into practical recommendations. Together with my subsequent SQL portfolio projects, they show the development of my skills from academic financial and statistical analysis towards practical, data-driven problem-solving.
